What We Do & Who We Are

Who We Are

We're Sunk Robotics, a student-run robotics team based in Medford, Massachusetts.

What We Do

From building ROVs to testing new robotic solutions, we tackle real-world challenges.

Our History

Since 2020, we've developed multiple underwater engineering solutions including STEVE, JENA, and JONA ROVs.

Where We've Been

We were invited to the ribbon cutting of MITRE's new R&D pool and demonstrated our robots.

Accomplishments

We have competed in several MATE ROV competitions, including regionals and world championships.

BOB ROV

Overview

BOB ROV is a state-of-the-art, vertically profiling autonomous robot designed to collect critical oceanographic data from the surface down to great depths. Built as part of the GO-BGC (Global Ocean Biogeochemistry Array) initiative, BOB ROV aims to help researchers monitor and understand the chemistry, biology, and physics of the global ocean in real time. Unlike traditional floats, BOB was engineered with real-world usability at its core. It features a next-generation PCB system, improving reliability, modularity, and serviceability in the field.
